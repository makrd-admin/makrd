# Product Vision — [WORKING TITLE]

_Read this for product rationale and strategy. Day-to-day build rules live in `CLAUDE.md`._

## One-liner
A community-owned peer-to-peer 3D printing ecosystem: get anything printed locally by other
makers, earn and spend points, and close the loop with recycling and finishing services —
eventually on the cheapest fully-modular printer on the market.

## The four pillars
1. **P2P printing platform** — the network/marketplace connecting idle printers with print
   jobs. This is the wedge and what v1 builds.
2. **Filament recycling** — turn failed prints and waste into cheap filament (~60% reclaimed
   and resold into the community). Lowers material cost for everyone → structural price
   advantage.
3. **Automated print finishing** — resin-coat / polish prints so network output competes
   with professional-grade finishes.
4. **100% modular printer** — the moonshot: cheap, upgrade-don't-replace hardware so
   hobbyists don't rebuy for every upgrade and businesses can tune a machine to their needs.

## The flywheel — why the pillars belong together
More members → more prints → more waste → recycling → cheaper material → cheaper prints →
more members. Finishing raises output quality and pulls in higher-value demand. The modular
printer seeds cheap supply back into the network. Each pillar strengthens the others. No
competitor owns the whole loop — that integration is the moat.

## Points / circular economy
- **Earn points:** fulfilling print jobs, recycling waste prints and filament, offering
  finishing/assembly services.
- **Spend points:** getting things printed, borrowing community tools (laser cutter, etc.),
  buying popular parts, finishing services.
- **Anti-fraud:** each printer has a unique model ID + secret code word; the system checks
  for duplicate/abusive accounts sharing one machine.

## Quality & logistics (later phases)
Post-print verification at the provider → computer-vision inspection at a small HQ → standby
HQ printers for jobs no one accepts → delivery.

## Competitive landscape (short version)
- **Marketplaces / aggregators** (Xometry, Craftcloud, Treatstock, Shapeways): aggregate
  professional bureaus; not community- or points-based.
- **P2P networks** (Project DIAMOnD, 3DOS, GreenGrid3D): mostly B2B / industrial or regional.
- **Recycling** (Filabot, ProtoCycler, Precious Plastic): sold as equipment or DIY, not an
  integrated networked service.
- **Finishing** (AMT PostPro, Zortrax Apoller, Xometry vapor smoothing): industrial machines
  or in-house bureau tools; automated resin finishing as an accessible service is unclaimed.
- **Modular printers** (Snapmaker multifunction, Prusa upgrade kits): premium or closed;
  Bambu is cheap but fully closed and non-modular. "Cheapest, truly modular" is unowned.

## Our edge
1. **The integrated loop** — nobody combines all four pillars.
2. **Circular economy as pricing power** — recycled material structurally undercuts cost.
3. **Community-first + points**, versus purely transactional marketplaces.
4. **Geography** — start in India (Bengaluru): a large, cost-sensitive, underserved maker /
   small-business base the US/EU incumbents don't serve.

## Sequencing
Platform first (validate demand, build community) → recycling + finishing (deepen the moat,
improve unit economics) → modular printer (fund and de-risk with proven demand).
